# D-012 · A 10 m planning horizon

- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Status:** Accepted

With ±15° and the relay ramp, the Tesla needs about 7 m to move 1.7 m
sideways. A shorter horizon (and a test obstacle at 3 m) made swerving
infeasible; 10 m reaches the far edge of perception and the avoidance test
uses an obstacle seen at ~8 m. The safety node uses the same 10 m.
