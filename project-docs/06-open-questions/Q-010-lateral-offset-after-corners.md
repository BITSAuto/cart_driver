# Q-010 · Lateral offset after corners

- **Opened:** 2026-10-08 · **Status:** Open · **Priority:** low

On the loop the car sits ~0.2 m inside on corners and up to 0.6 m outside
where a straight follows a corner, the same every lap (mean 0.31 m, max
0.93 m). Probably the 1.5° candidate spacing plus smoothness cost; try finer
candidates or a higher route weight.
