# Q-015 · Keeping left without a route

- **Opened:** 2026-10-15 · **Status:** Open · **Priority:** medium

The road-centre cost aims for the middle of the whole road, which on a
two-way road means the centre line. Indian roads drive on the left. Options:
offset the target line towards the left edge (by a lane width, or a fraction
of the measured road width), or follow the left road edge at a fixed
distance when only that edge is visible. Single-lane campus paths may need
no offset.
